Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Pixel Animation Studio Ultra Pro, accessible from your web browser, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by our platform and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

🔒 Client-Side File Processing Notice

Your creative privacy matters to us. Pixel Animation Studio Ultra Pro is a browser-based client application. All canvas manipulations, animations, sprite sheet generations, image-to-pixel conversions, and color palette extractions occur entirely inside your browser local engine.

We do not upload, transmit, or store your custom artwork, image files, or sprite designs to external cloud servers. Your files remain entirely on your own computer device.

1. Log Files

Like most standard web hosting environments, our hosting partner (Vercel) utilizes automated web log infrastructure. These files merely log visitors when they access web pages. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and data click counts. This analytical profile information is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Our website uses "cookies" strictly to optimize operational performance and store structural user layout settings. These cookies are utilized to store system preferences and track which unique features or tool panels our visitors interact with most frequently.

3. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Disclosures

We have integrated Google Analytics (a web assessment service provided by Google LLC) to collect insights regarding user interaction data. Google Analytics uses temporary cookies to analyze traffic patterns and trace visitor sessions.

Through this tracker integration, Google automatically captures device properties, browser variations, geographic approximations (such as country/city), and real-time site behaviors (such as active button clicks or sheet generation counts). This collected metrics footprint allows us to prioritize tool bug fixes and improve features layout.

You can read how Google processes data directly via Google's Privacy & Terms. Visitors can fully prevent their data from being evaluated by downloading and running the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on tool.

4. Third Party Privacy Frameworks

Our Privacy Policy framework does not apply to other software distributors or external target web links. We explicitly recommend you cross-check the respective Privacy Policies of these individual external platforms for comprehensive technical directives regarding analytical data opting out boundaries.

5. Children's Information Safeguards

Another primary focus layer is adding protection mechanisms for children while navigating internet utilities. We strongly encourage parents or guardians to actively observe, guide, or monitor online web activities. Pixel Animation Studio Ultra Pro does not knowingly extract or harbor any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age threshold of 13.

6. Online Privacy Policy Bounds

This privacy provision layout applies exclusively to our active online application utilities and remains valid for visitors navigating our public web destination regarding tracking parameters shared or collected. This policy is completely invalid regarding background data channels handled offline.

7. Consent Authorization

By interacting natively with our design suite tools and production workspace environment, you hereby fully acknowledge your consent regarding our formal Privacy Policy provisions and agree explicitly to its technical terms.

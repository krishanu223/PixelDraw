How to Use the Creative Tools

1. Creating & Animating

Set the Grid

Pick your standard asset resolution (such as 16x16, 32x32, or 64x64 pixels) to start crisp.

Manage Frames

Use the timeline panel below the canvas to add, duplicate, or delete animation frames.

Toggle Onion Skin

Enable onion skinning to see a faint silhouette of your previous frame, ensuring flawless motion tracking.

2. Smart Conversion & Palettes

Image-to-Pixel Conversion

Drag and drop any regular reference photo straight into the converter tool to downscale it into dynamic, editable pixel art.

Palette Extraction

Upload source graphics to instantly extract cohesive, harmonious color color palettes, ensuring aesthetic consistency across your game world.

3. Final Production Export

Sprite Sheet Configuration

Arrange your horizontal and vertical frame columns symmetrically before generating the file.

Download & Deploy

Hit the export button to instantly download transparent PNG sheets or looping GIF formats ready to import directly into your project build.
